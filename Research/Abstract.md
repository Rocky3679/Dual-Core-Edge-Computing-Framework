# Abstract

The integration of highly variable industrial loads into modern microgrid environments necessitates rapid power quality regulation and intelligent energy distribution. Traditional centralized energy management systems often suffer from significant network propagation delays, which can hinder effective reactive power compensation during transient events.

This project presents an ESP32-based edge computing framework for simultaneous Power Factor Correction (PFC) and Load Demand Management (LDM). The system combines FreeRTOS task scheduling, neural network-assisted decision making, MQTT cloud telemetry, and renewable energy source management.

Experimental validation using MATLAB/Simulink and a hardware prototype demonstrated fast response, improved power factor, reduced latency, and reliable autonomous microgrid operation.
